# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do **not** open a public issue for security problems.

Report them privately through
[GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/wuplayapp/wuplay-releases/security/advisories/new)
with:

- Affected version (see Settings → About)
- Device and Android version
- Steps to reproduce or a proof of concept
- Impact you believe the issue has

You can expect an initial reply within a few days. Once a fix is available, a
release note will describe the issue in general terms and credit you if you want
to be named.

## Scope

In scope: the WuPlay APK published in this repository's releases, its update
mechanism, the web configurator and the multi-device sync protocol.

Out of scope: third-party Stremio add-ons and the content they return, issues
that require a rooted or otherwise compromised device, and reports about
copyrighted material (see the README for how WuPlay relates to content).

## Safe harbor

Good-faith research that follows this policy will not be pursued or reported.
Please avoid privacy violations, data destruction and service disruption while
testing.
